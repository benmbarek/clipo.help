FAQ

Quick answers

Do I need to create an account to use Clipo?

No. Install Clipo and start saving immediately—no email, no login, and no cloud sync required.

Does Clipo download videos from social networks?

No. Clipo is a personal bookmark and link organizer. It saves the link, clean preview card, and your personal notes locally so you can jump straight back to the original Reel, video, or post whenever you have time.

Can I browse my saved list without internet?

Yes! Your saved feed, thumbnails, titles, folders, notes, and instant search all live locally on your phone, so you can browse on flights, subways, or anywhere offline.

How do I move my saved posts to a new phone?

Open Settings inside Clipo and tap Export Backup to save a .json or .zip file anywhere on your phone or Drive, then import it in one tap on your new device.

Is Clipo free?

Yes, Clipo is completely free to use, supported by non-intrusive Google AdMob ads with built-in GDPR privacy controls. Your saved bookmarks are never shared with advertisers.
